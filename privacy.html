Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SKILL//ISSUE, published by Cortisol Club, handles information when you use the game.

Google Sign-In and Google Play Games Services

SKILL//ISSUE uses Google services, including Google Sign-In and Google Play Games Services, to provide features such as player authentication and online leaderboards.

When using these features, Google may process information associated with your Google account, player profile and gameplay activity, including scores submitted to leaderboards.

Authentication is handled by Google. SKILL//ISSUE does not receive or store your Google account password.

Information processed through Google services is handled according to Google's own privacy policies and the permissions granted by the user.

Information We Collect

SKILL//ISSUE does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information directly from users.

Certain technical, account-related or gameplay information may be processed by third-party services used by the game in order to provide authentication, leaderboard functionality, security, diagnostics or other platform features.

Leaderboards

SKILL//ISSUE uses Google Play Games Services to provide online leaderboard functionality.

When a player submits a score, information associated with their Google Play Games profile and leaderboard score may be processed by Google in order to display rankings and provide leaderboard features.

SKILL//ISSUE does not independently store Google account passwords or other authentication credentials.

Third-Party Services

SKILL//ISSUE currently uses Google Play Games Services for authentication and leaderboard functionality.

These services may process account identifiers, player profile information, gameplay activity and leaderboard scores where required to provide those features.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

Reasonable steps are taken to minimise the amount of personal information handled directly by SKILL//ISSUE and to rely on established platform services where authentication or account functionality is required.

Children's Privacy

SKILL//ISSUE does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Google account services and Google Play Games Services may apply their own age requirements, parental controls and account restrictions.

Advertising

SKILL//ISSUE does not currently use in-game advertising.

Advertising services may be added in a future version of the game. If advertising is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how advertising providers may process data and what choices are available to users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SKILL//ISSUE, its features, or the third-party services it uses change.

Any changes will be reflected on this page together with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy can be directed to Cortisol Club through the contact information provided on the official SKILL//ISSUE Google Play store listing.
