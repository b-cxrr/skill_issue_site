Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SKILL//ISSUE, published by Cortisol Club, handles information when you use the game.

Information We Handle

SKILL//ISSUE does not ask users to directly provide sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, home addresses or identification documents.

Certain account, technical and gameplay information may be processed through third-party services used by SKILL//ISSUE in order to provide features such as authentication, leaderboards, security and platform functionality.

Google Sign-In and Google Play Games Services

SKILL//ISSUE uses Google services, including Google Sign-In and Google Play Games Services, to provide player authentication and connected game features.

When you use these features, Google may process information associated with your Google account or Google Play Games profile. This may include account identifiers, player profile information and gameplay-related information required to provide those services.

Authentication is handled by Google. SKILL//ISSUE does not receive or store your Google account password.

Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's own privacy policies, account settings and terms.

Leaderboards

SKILL//ISSUE uses Google Play Games Services to provide online leaderboard functionality.

When you submit a score, information associated with your Google Play Games profile and your submitted score may be processed by Google in order to display rankings and provide leaderboard functionality.

Leaderboard information may be visible to other players in accordance with the visibility and privacy settings provided by Google Play Games Services.

Third-Party Services

SKILL//ISSUE currently uses Google Play Games Services and related Google platform services for authentication and leaderboard functionality.

These services are operated by Google and may process information according to their own privacy policies.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising

SKILL//ISSUE does not currently use in-game advertising.

Advertising services may be introduced in a future version of the game. If advertising is added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant advertising provider, the information that may be processed and the privacy choices available to users.

Data Retention

Information processed by third-party services such as Google is retained according to the policies and settings of those services.

Cortisol Club does not independently maintain a database of Google account passwords or authentication credentials.

Data Security

Cortisol Club takes reasonable steps to minimise the amount of personal information handled directly by SKILL//ISSUE and relies on established platform services where authentication or account functionality is required.

However, no electronic system or method of data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may manage permissions, account settings and privacy options associated with Google services through your Google account and Google Play Games settings where available.

You may also contact Cortisol Club if you have questions about how SKILL//ISSUE handles information.

Children's Privacy

SKILL//ISSUE does not knowingly request or collect personal information directly from children.

Google account services and Google Play Games Services may apply their own age requirements, parental controls and account restrictions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SKILL//ISSUE, its features or the third-party services it uses change.

Any changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
